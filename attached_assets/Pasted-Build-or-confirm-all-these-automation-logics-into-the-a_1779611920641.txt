Build or confirm all these automation logics into the app. API keys will be stored in Replit Secrets as OPENAI_API_KEY, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and RESEND_API_KEY. Use those secret variables — do not hardcode any keys.
Use OpenAI API for all WhatsApp automations and Claude API only for care summary and wellness newsletter. Use Resend for all email delivery.
Pipeline State Transitions
	∙	Receptionist checks patient into queue → patient moves to Queued
	∙	Nurse logs treatment plan → patient moves to In Care
	∙	Treatment plan duration ends → patient moves to Post Treatment
	∙	Post Treatment check-in period ends (hospital configured) → patient moves to Post Care
	∙	Patient inactive for hospital configured dormancy period → patient moves to Dormant
	∙	Dormant patient checks in → patient moves to Queued
	∙	Patient removed from queue → patient returns to previous state before queue
	∙	If previous state was Booked → patient returns to state before Booked
	∙	If previous state was Dormant → patient moves to Post Care instead
Queue Messages (WhatsApp - OpenAI - Warm tone)
	∙	Patient added to queue → send their queue number and a warm message letting them know they are seen and being taken care of
	∙	Queue number keeps updating as queue moves → send updated position so patient always feels informed and seen
	∙	Messages must feel warm and human not robotic
In Care - Care Summary (WhatsApp - Claude API)
	∙	When nurse logs treatment plan → send patient a care summary
	∙	Care summary must explain their care process clearly, reassure them, and let them know the team will be with them through the process
	∙	Do not mention their diagnosis or sickness
	∙	Tone must be warm and reassuring
	∙	Use ANTHROPIC_API_KEY
Post Treatment - Recovery Phase (WhatsApp - OpenAI)
	∙	Regular warm check-in messages sent based on hospital configured frequency
	∙	Messages must feel genuinely caring like the hospital is truly checking on them as a person not just a patient
	∙	Patient must feel seen not just treated
	∙	Use OPENAI_API_KEY
Post Care - Wellness Phase (WhatsApp - OpenAI)
	∙	Mostly wellness focused messages
	∙	Encouraging and uplifting tone
	∙	Based on hospital configured frequency
	∙	Use OPENAI_API_KEY
Dormant Patients
	∙	Dormant patients receive the same weekly wellness newsletter as active patients
	∙	No separate dormant message
Missed Visit (Manual)
	∙	Nurse voluntarily flags a patient who missed their expected visit
	∙	This is not automatic
	∙	Flagged patient appears in receptionist call tasks
Call Tasks - Receptionist Automated Message
	∙	Receptionist sees flagged patient in call tasks
	∙	Receptionist chooses to send an automated WhatsApp message
	∙	OpenAI reads the reason for the flag and automatically chooses the best tone and generates the most appropriate message for that specific patient situation
	∙	Use OPENAI_API_KEY
Appointments (If Module Active)
	∙	Appointment booked → WhatsApp confirmation sent to patient
	∙	24 hours before appointment → WhatsApp reminder sent
	∙	2 hours before appointment → WhatsApp final reminder sent
	∙	Appointment missed → WhatsApp follow up message sent
	∙	Use OPENAI_API_KEY
Feedback (Email - OpenAI)
	∙	End of day → all patients seen that day receive an email with a link to their unique feedback form
	∙	Feedback form collects: overall experience, wait time, staff friendliness, quality of care, would you recommend us — all rated 1 to 5 stars — plus optional comments
	∙	Form submissions save directly to Supabase
	∙	Admin feedback dashboard reads and displays live feedback data in real time
	∙	Each hospital sends feedback emails from their own configured email address
	∙	Use OPENAI_API_KEY to generate email content and RESEND_API_KEY to deliver
Wellness Newsletter (Email - Claude API)
	∙	Weekly → Claude API generates wellness newsletter based on hospital configured tone and active departments
	∙	Newsletter covers one topic per week rotating so the same topic is never repeated until full cycle is complete
	∙	Topics are general wellness awareness shaped by the hospital’s active departments
	∙	Each newsletter is detailed and explanatory covering: what the topic is, why it matters, common causes or triggers, benefits of good practice, what to avoid, and one simple action step for the week
	∙	Written in the hospital’s configured tone
	∙	Admin can review and edit the newsletter before it sends
	∙	Admin can optionally add a YouTube or TikTok link to support the tip
	∙	Sent via email to all active and dormant patients
	∙	Each hospital sends newsletter from their own configured email address
	∙	Use ANTHROPIC_API_KEY to generate content and RESEND_API_KEY to deliver
Email Service
	∙	Use Resend as the email delivery service using RESEND_API_KEY
	∙	Each hospital has their own sending email address configured in Super Admin
	∙	When a hospital is configured their email domain is verified through Resend
	∙	All patient emails go out from that hospital’s own email address
