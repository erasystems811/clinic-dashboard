Make the following changes to Era Patient automation and messaging system:
REMOVE COMPLETELY:
Remove the WhatsApp Messages inbox feature entirely. Remove all pipeline stage notification messages that tell patients what stage they are in like you are now in post treatment or your care plan has started — these should never be sent. Remove the AI generation for appointment messages, feedback messages, queue messages, post care messages, dormant messages and post treatment messages.
QUEUE MESSAGES — WhatsApp or SMS based on hospital config — Templated:
Queue messages are now milestone based only. Send only these 4 messages and nothing else. First when patient is added to queue send their queue number with a warm welcome message. Second when they are next in line send a heads up message. Third when it is their turn send a come in now message. Fourth if wait time exceeds 35 minutes and the patient has not moved send a general apology message to all patients still waiting. No other queue updates. All queue messages must end with a closed statement so patients know not to reply.
CARE PLAN — Split between WhatsApp or SMS and Email:
When nurse logs a treatment plan send two messages. First send a short WhatsApp or SMS message (based on hospital config) that is templated saying their care plan has started and to check their email for full details. Example: ‘Your care plan at GISD Hospital has started. Please check your email for your full care plan details.’ This message is templated not AI generated. Second send a detailed care plan explanation email generated by OpenAI API. Direct OpenAI to be efficient and concise. The email must explain their care process clearly, reassure them, never mention diagnosis, use the hospital configured tone. Care plan email ends with closed statement but includes a line inviting them to reach out directly if needed. This same care plan email is also resent when the treatment plan is changed or edited.
POST TREATMENT CHECK-INS — Email — Templated:
Send exactly 3 check-in emails only. Day 1 after treatment ends, Day 4 after treatment ends, Day 7 after treatment ends. Each day has its own warm templated message. Day 1 feels like a gentle first check-in. Day 4 feels like a caring mid-point check-in. Day 7 feels like a warm final recovery check-in. All 3 end with closed statement but include invitation to reach out directly if needed. No other post treatment messages.
POST CARE — Email — Templated:
After 30 days in Post Care state send one templated wellness email. Warm, encouraging, checking in on their overall wellbeing. Closed statement ending.
DORMANT — Email — Templated:
After 250 days of inactivity send one templated email. The tone must feel like the hospital is just saying hello with no pressure. Something like: you have not been around in a while and that is actually good news — it means you have been staying well. We just wanted to check in and say hi. We hope you are taking care of yourself and staying on top of your health. We are always here whenever you need us. Closed statement ending. Never guilt-inducing.
APPOINTMENT MESSAGES — Email — Templated:
All appointment messages go to email only. Templated not AI generated. Send confirmation when booked, reminder 24 hours before, reminder 2 hours before, follow up if missed. All end with closed statement.
FEEDBACK — Email — Templated:
End of day feedback form email is templated not AI generated. Goes to email only. Closed statement ending.
WELLNESS NEWSLETTER — Email — Claude API:
Weekly wellness newsletter generated by Claude API goes to email only. Admin can regenerate maximum 5 times per week. Limit the regenerate button to 5 uses per week per hospital. Newsletter ends with closed statement.
CALL TASKS:
Automated message from call task is limited to maximum 5 automated messages per day per hospital. Manual message from call task goes to email only and is marked as important. Nurse flagged automated message goes to WhatsApp or SMS based on hospital configuration. The message is generated by OpenAI based on the reason for the flag. The receptionist can edit the generated message before sending. Ends with closed statement.
ALL MESSAGES RULE:
Every single message in the system must end with a closed statement so patients know not to reply. Where there is need for patient to reach out include a line like if you have any questions please do not hesitate to contact us directly but make clear it is not an open reply thread.
CHANNEL CONFIGURATION:
Add a configuration option in Super Admin hospital settings where the hospital picks their notification channel — WhatsApp or SMS. If they pick WhatsApp use Twilio WhatsApp API. If they pick SMS use Termii SMS API. Email is always active for every hospital regardless of their choice. Queue messages, care plan onboarding notification and nurse flagged messages only go to WhatsApp or SMS based on hospital configuration. All other messages go to email.
SUMMARY OF CHANNELS:
WhatsApp or SMS based on hospital config handles: queue milestone messages, care plan started notification templated, nurse flagged automated messages generated by OpenAI and editable by receptionist.
Email handles: care plan full explanation generated by OpenAI, post treatment check-ins day 1 day 4 and day 7 templated, post care 30 day message templated, dormant 250 day message templated, appointment confirmation and reminders templated, feedback form templated, wellness newsletter generated by Claude API, manual call task messages.
Remove OpenAI from all templated messages. OpenAI is only used for care plan email explanation and nurse flagged automated messages. Claude API is only used for wellness newsletter. All other messages are fixed templates.
