PIPELINE STATES: Six states only. Booked — appointment scheduled, reminders running. Queued — patient physically at clinic waiting, queue notifications running. In Care — active treatment plan logged by nurse, treatment reminders running. Post Treatment — treatment recently complete, recovery check-ins running for 2 to 3 weeks. Post Care — no active care, receives weekly wellness newsletter only, visible on dashboard. Dormant — patient hasn’t visited in a configurable time period set by the hospital.

PATIENT REGISTRATION: First visit requires full registration — name, phone number, email, age, gender. This creates a permanent record. All subsequent visits the receptionist searches by name or ID. System displays their phone and email for verbal reconfirmation. Receptionist edits if anything changed. Then clicks Add to Queue.

QUEUE MANAGEMENT: This is a core module, not optional. Every hospital gets it. The Queue Management tab shows the live queue with position number, patient name, time added, and how long they have been waiting. Receptionist adds patients to queue from this tab. Receptionist ticks a checkbox to remove a patient from the queue when the doctor calls them in. Queue automatically updates positions and sends WhatsApp notifications to remaining patients when someone is removed.

NURSE WORKFLOW: Nurse searches patient by name or ID. Opens their record. Logs treatment plan with medication timing (morning, afternoon, evening, night, any combination), treatment type (medication, injection, dressing, monitoring, physical therapy), and duration in days. Saving the treatment plan automatically moves the patient to In Care state and starts treatment reminder automations.

RECEPTIONIST ACCESS: Receptionist sees only Queue Management tab and Call Tasks tab. Queue Management handles live queue, patient registration, adding to queue, and checkbox removal from queue. Call Tasks shows a simple list of patients flagged for personal calls with patient name, phone number, reason for call, and a field to log the call outcome. 
Receptionist cannot access patient history, pipeline, communication logs, or treatment plans.

APPOINTMENT MODULE: This is optional. If activated by the hospital, the receptionist also sees an Appointments tab where she can create, view, edit, and cancel bookings. If not activated she does not see this tab.

Nurse has her own tab called Nurse Station. It contains two things only. First, a treatment plan input section where she searches a patient by name or ID and logs their treatment plan — medication timing per type(if they have both type)(morning, afternoon and night), treatment type(drugs they can take on their own or coming to hospital for treatment or both, and duration(how long treatment is for). Second, a flag section where she can flag patients who are In Care but missed their expected treatment visit(if it requires coming to hospital), to trigger either an automated check-in message or a personal call to confirm they are okay.

Patient state transitions are automatic based on these rules. When nurse logs a treatment plan the patient automatically moves to In Care. When the treatment plan duration ends the patient automatically moves to Post Treatment. After the Post Treatment check-in period configured by the hospital ends the patient automatically moves to Post Care. When a patient has not visited the clinic for the hospital-configured dormancy period they automatically move to Dormant. When a dormant patient checks in again they automatically return to Queued. When receptionist checks a patient into the queue they move to Queued state. These transitions happen automatically without any manual intervention except the initial triggers — nurse logging treatment plan and receptionist checking patient in.

When a patient is removed from the queue by the receptionist they automatically return to whatever pipeline state they were in before they joined the queue. Eg if a patient was post care before checking in they return to post care after leaving the queue(except if the patient was dormant then they move to post visit) until the nurse logs a treatment plan during that visit. If the nurse logs a treatment plan they move to In Care regardless of what their previous state was. The queue is a temporary state that overlays the patient’s existing pipeline state. It never permanently replaces it. 

Receptionist access depends on activated modules. If Appointment Booking module is activated, she also sees an Appointments tab where she can create, view, edit and cancel bookings. If the module is not activated, she does not see this tab. Same logic applies to any future module — receptionist only sees tabs for modules the hospital has activated.
